Your pocket guide to the creatures of the ocean

About the App

Marine Life Explorer is an educational Progressive Web App that teaches you about fascinating ocean creatures. Select any animal from the menu to see a photo, read a description, and hear an audio clip. Works on any device and can be installed for offline use!

How to Install

On Desktop (Chrome):

  1. Open the app in Google Chrome
  2. Look for the install icon in the address bar (a computer with a + sign)
  3. Click it and select Install
  4. The app will open as its own window on your computer

On Mobile (Android/iPhone):

  1. Open the app in your mobile browser
  2. Tap the browser menu (three dots or share icon)
  3. Select Add to Home Screen
  4. The app icon will appear on your home screen
